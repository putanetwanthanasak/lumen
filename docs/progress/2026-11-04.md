### Bugs found
- **The mock listed a move under a new id, and its amount as sent** (review F1). `POST /buffer/move-to-main` returned `transaction_id` X while `GET /bank/main-account` listed id Y; `"1500"` was listed as `"1500"`, where the backend reads back `"1500.00"`.
  - Root cause: `recordMovement` made its own `crypto.randomUUID()` and stored `b.amount` unchanged.
  - Fix: the move passes its `transaction_id`, and the amount goes through `fromSatang(toSatang(...))`.
  - Proving test: `UI_62_a_move_is_listed_under_its_transaction_id_with_the_amount_as_stored`, which failed before the fix.
