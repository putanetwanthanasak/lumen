- **A guard spelled out the banned names** (review round 1 F1). `mainAccount.test.ts` had the names literally in its UI-61 pattern.

  - A move commits before its credit, so a row whose credit never happened was counted in the opened balance. The retry then found its key unknown to the new bank and credited again.

- **The served app had two banks** (review round 2 F2). The webhook processor reads `get_bank_provider()` directly, so it saw the old singleton.
  - Fix: open the singleton itself. Proving test: the served KD_04 tests (B214).
