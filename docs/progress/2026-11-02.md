### Done
- **`frontend/src/barSegments.ts` (new).** It draws a month's bar as parts, left to right:
  - `ok` (green) up to the budget;
  - `buffer` (teal, `BUFFER_BAR`): what was moved for the month, never past the shortfall;
  - `gap`: the API's `remaining_gap`, amber, or red over one third of income.

  How each state is drawn:
  - not prepared: ok, gap;
  - partly prepared: ok, buffer, gap;
  - prepared: ok, buffer.

  Details:
  - Positions come from `barPermille`. The only arithmetic is on per-mille numbers, never on money.
  - In a partly prepared month, the gap's start is held at the budget mark (`t - g < b ? b : t - g`), so the teal part never runs backwards. A tiny move can draw a zero-width teal part; the line still says what was moved.
  - Partly prepared with `remaining_gap` "0.00" (never sent by the API) is drawn as prepared.
  - Over or within budget is the API's `over_budget` flag.
  - A month within budget but over a third of income is one red part (review F1).
  - `components/BarParts.tsx` draws the parts on both screens. Tests: UI-52.
- **Home's bar** reads this month's own row of `GET /buffer/shortfalls` (`HeroView.barRow`), never the first month over budget. The buffer line is unchanged and still names the first month over (open question 3, answered). Tests: UI-53.

  - Chip: a month the buffer fully prepared gets "กันชนเตรียมเงินแล้ว" (shield, `BUFFER_CHIP`); partly prepared keeps "เกินงบ".
  - Over one third of income always keeps "เกินเกณฑ์ที่ ธปท. แนะนำ" and a red ring on the bar (`ring-2 ring-bar-risk`), whatever the buffer state (open question 4, answered).
