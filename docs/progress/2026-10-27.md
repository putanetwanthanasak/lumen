   - Fix (`06dd5d2`): `BankAccountProvider.applied_to_savings(user, key)` returns what a key already moved; the run records that amount as `leftover_saved`, and the audit detail has `recovered: true`.

2. **Review round 2 F1: a buffer move whose commit was lost could credit the main account twice.**

The steps: move "a" (500.00, the whole buffer) loses its commit after the bank credit; move "b" (500.00) then succeeds; the retry of "a" gets 422.
   - Root cause: the main account was credited before the commit, so a lost commit left money credited with no ledger debit. Later moves were checked against a ledger balance that still counted it.

### Bugs found (PR A)
1. **Review F1: the 600-month cap cut off months over budget.**
   - Reproduced: 6,000.00 a month for 600 months from 2027-06, with a budget of 5,000.00. It gave back_in_budget 2076-11 instead of 2077-06, a shortfall of 593,000.00 instead of 600,000.00, and the buffer "covered" while it was 5,000.00 short.

   - Fix (`ec71984`): spans of equal months over the whole range, with each total from `month_totals`; only the rows are capped (`months_truncated`).
   - Proved by `test_AS_02_months_over_budget_past_the_cap_still_count`, `test_AS_05_a_run_past_the_cap_*` and the uncapped AS-10 oracle (B74).
2. **Review F2: safe_new_monthly could get RED from the preview.**
   - Reproduced: income 12,000.00 and budget 5,000.00, the case L64 warns about. The plan said 5,000.00 was safe, and the preview of 5,000.00 x 12 said RED.
   - Root cause: the plan never received income, so "safe" meant within budget only.
   - Fix (`ec71984`): headroom is capped at a third of income, rounded down to the satang.
   - Proved by `test_AS_03_safe_new_monthly_previews_without_red_when_the_budget_is_above_a_third` and AS-09 (B75).
   - This tightens your definition ("min headroom"); see Open questions.
3. **Round 2 review F1: a huge principal crashed the plan before its own guard.**
   - Reproduced: a principal above 1E14 with a 1-month term raised `decimal.InvalidOperation` (a 500).
   - Root cause: the payment was computed before the size check, and 34 digits cannot hold a payment of 1E14 or more to 20 places.
   - Fix (`2d00871`): a principal above MONEY_MAX is `ESTIMATE_TOO_LARGE` before any payment math. Proved by B77.
4. **Found while building: Decimal noise could round the payment up a satang too far.** 4.00 over one month at 6% a year is exactly 4.02, but the 34-digit computation gives 4.0200...004, which ROUND_UP turns into 4.03. A brute-force search found 23 such cases among 8,500 tried. Fix: clean to 20 places, then round up. Proved by the 4.02 case (B68).
5. **Test-side, fixed in the tests and not by loosening:**
