### PR1 `fix/per-user-balance-lock`

#### Done
- **R3:** `savings._lock_balance` runs before the safety-net read. It does `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on `user_balances`, then `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on that row. A second purchase for the same user waits there until the first one commits, and its bank transfer happens before that commit. So the second purchase reads the bank after the first purchase's money has moved. `_credit`'s optimistic version check is unchanged and stays as a second layer.
