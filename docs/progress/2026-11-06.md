### Deviations from docs/spec and why
- **The saving rule in the task ("10% of item value, min 10, max 500 THB") is L46/L52, superseded by L63** (percent or fixed, no minimum or cap, the 500.00 safety net). The form computes no saving: the backend applies the user's own L63 setting to the price the form sends. Nothing in the saving rule changed. ม่อน saves 10%, so the test numbers read as 10%.
- **DESIGN.md 3.6 said the client never computes money.** It now allows one exact product, monthly x months in BigInt satang, in `money.ts` (UI-51 still keeps arithmetic out of every other file). The task asked for integer satang; the other way to do it, a new endpoint, would change the API contract.
- **UI-18's "an edited value is never overwritten"** now holds while the monthly amount is the input. Typing the price again makes the price the input, and the API's answer then replaces the monthly amount (the task's rule 1).
- **The monthly amount's help** "คิดแบบผ่อน 0% ถ้าแอปแจ้งยอดต่อเดือนไม่ตรงนี้ แก้ให้ตรงกับแอปได้" shows only before anything is calculated and while the price is the input. Once the monthly amount is calculated, the note takes its place (one line under the calculated field). When the monthly amount is the input, it is not shown, because it would say "0%".
- **The recorded demo** (`kidnee-demo-phone.mp4`, scene 3, 0:09.0 to 0:18.7) shows the old two-line help under ค่างวดต่อเดือน; the new build shows the one-line note there. The rest of the scene is the same (`scene3-compare.png`). Not re-recorded; the task allows the note to differ.
