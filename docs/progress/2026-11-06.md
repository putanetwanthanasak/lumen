### Deviations from docs/spec and why
- **The printed line's date is "06 พ.ย. 69", not "05 พ.ย. 69"** as in v1. `created_at` is Postgres `now()`, and the seed dates its rows on the day it runs (its months are relative to today), so a reset on Nov 6 prints Nov 6, and the move during the take is dated Nov 6 too. Keeping Nov 5 would need hand-edited rows in `lumen_kidnee`, and even then the move would be dated Nov 6. Every amount, label, month and scene is the same.
